Winterization, planned around your last lake trip

When you're done on the water for the year, proper winterization protects your engine and systems through the off-season.

Platinum Service Customers: winterize and de-winterize are included at no charge.

Request Winterization

Scheduling and availability are confirmed directly with the shop.

  1. Plan around your last trip

    Time winterization for after your final outing of the year so your boat goes straight from the water into the off-season protected.

  2. Reach out before you haul in

    Contact Andrew before bringing the boat to the shop so the work can be lined up ahead of your drop-off.

  3. Share your boat's info

    Include the year, make, and engine details so the shop can confirm the scope of the work and timing with you.

Good to know

A few shop policies so there are no surprises. We will always go over specifics with you at drop-off.

Half-hour minimum
All repairs are billed with a half-hour minimum.
Off-site repairs
Repairs done away from the shop include a service call fee.
Special orders
Special-order returns are subject to a restocking fee.
Pickup within 15 days
Units not picked up within 15 days of completion accrue a daily storage fee. All units are stored outside, so we recommend leaving a cover at drop-off.
Model year
We do not service units built before 2005.
