Heading into a holiday weekend?

Lake shops get busy fast. The best move is to reach out early and give us enough information to understand the problem.

  1. Get in the queue early

    Memorial Day, July 4th, and Labor Day can fill the schedule quickly. Use the service request below before the rush builds.

  2. Get a straight answer

    Call or text the shop and we will tell you what information we need and what the next practical step looks like.

  3. Send the details

    Include the boat or PWC, engine, symptoms, and whether it is in the water or on a trailer. Better information means a faster first response.
