BEFORE YOU DROP OFF

Good to know

A few shop policies so there are no surprises. We will always go over specifics with you at drop-off.

Half-hour minimum

All repairs are billed with a half-hour minimum.

Off-site repairs

Repairs done away from the shop include a service call fee.

Special orders

Special-order returns are subject to a restocking fee.

Pickup within 15 days

Units not picked up within 15 days of completion accrue a daily storage fee. All units are stored outside, so we recommend leaving a cover at drop-off.

Model year

We do not service units built before 2005.
